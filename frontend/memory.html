How Hindsight Memory Works

This AI SOC Agent uses Hindsight to store and retrieve past security investigations. Unlike traditional databases that just store rows of data, Hindsight allows our AI Investigator to understand the context of previous attacks and learn from Analyst Feedback.

When you click "Investigate" on an alert, the AI automatically recalls similar past incidents and embeds them directly into its analysis report. This prevents the AI from repeating mistakes and helps it detect coordinated campaigns over time.
